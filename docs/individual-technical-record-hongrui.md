**How it works.** `GameFlow` reads the editable `WeekPlan` and asks `MicrogameCatalog` to instantiate each round. It connects `time_changed` to the HUD, `round_completed` to the score/life decision, and `result_finished` to navigation after feedback. On success the player gains 100 points; on failure they lose one life and retry after the bike transition, or reach game over at zero lives. A successful round advances to the next game, then to day clear when that day's games are finished. The flow reserves each result before feedback and checks a run token, preventing duplicate or stale callbacks from changing score or lives twice. I kept games behind this shared signal contract so the HUD and menus do not depend on an individual game's UI.
